Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, broad sweeps, pushing a surface or panel closed, OR rotating a knob, faucet, or spout in place (a steady turn that stays in one spot is free motion even though the gripper rests on it). Answer NO when the gripper is in delicate contact: grasping an object, inserting or aligning it precisely, OR when the gripper is hooked on a door handle or drawer pull and dragging that whole panel open along its path — a hinged door swinging out toward the robot, or a drawer sliding out. Pushing something shut is free motion (YES); gripping and hauling a door or drawer open is constrained contact (NO). A knob, faucet, or spout that merely spins in place is NOT a panel being hauled open — keep turning it YES. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
