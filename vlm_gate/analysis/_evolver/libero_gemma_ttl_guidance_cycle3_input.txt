Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, carrying a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps. A gripper that has ALREADY closed around an object and is now lifting or transporting it is NOT delicate contact: the grasp is finished, so keep answering YES through the whole transit, including a grasped object carried high above the table across open space toward a distant area, well clear of any surface or target. Answer NO only at the brief delicate instants: fingers actively closing to take a grasp, or the final descent where an object is being set down onto / aligned with / inserted into a specific spot.
