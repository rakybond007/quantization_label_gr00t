Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object across open space, retracting, broad sweeps, pushing a single surface or panel shut, OR rotating a knob, faucet, or spout in place (a steady turn that stays in one spot is free motion even though the gripper rests on it). Answer NO when the gripper is in delicate contact: grasping an object, inserting or aligning it precisely, when a grasped object is being threaded INTO or lifted OUT OF a deep recessed enclosure whose interior walls frame the gripper in the view — a sink basin, a microwave cavity, the inside of a narrow shelf — so the gripper must carefully clear those surrounding walls (navigating an enclosure's interior is contact-sensitive, not open transport; the moment those walls leave the frame and the object is over an open countertop, stovetop, or open shelf, it is free transport again — YES), when the gripper is hooked on a door handle or drawer pull and dragging that whole panel open along its path (a hinged door swinging out toward the robot, or a drawer sliding out), OR when TWO door panels are BOTH SWINGING — two independently moving leaves traveling toward each other so their free edges converge on a shared center seam to meet and align (the final mating of two moving leaves is a precise terminal contact, not a free push). The decisive signal is TWO leaves IN MOTION at once: only when both panels are actively swinging toward each other does this NO apply. A SINGLE moving panel whose leading edge approaches a STATIONARY frame, jamb, or wall is ordinary push-shut — answer YES — even if a second already-closed door, fixed panel, or wall is visible beside it; one moving leaf against something that is not moving is free push, not a two-leaf mating. A knob, faucet, or spout that merely spins in place is NOT a panel being hauled open — keep turning it YES. Placing or releasing onto an OPEN, unwalled surface (a countertop, a stovetop, an open shelf) is still free transport — YES; the enclosure NO applies only while surrounding interior walls box in the gripper. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
