Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, broad sweeps, or pushing a panel closed away from the robot. Answer NO when the gripper is in delicate contact: grasping an object, or inserting/aligning it precisely. Also lean NO when the gripper is engaged on a handle or knob and pulling a hinged door or sliding drawer OPEN toward the robot — the arm is then moving under load along a fixed hinge/slide path, a constrained motion that should be protected (pushing a panel CLOSED away from the robot is not constrained and stays YES). Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
