Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, broad sweeps, or pushing a panel closed away from the robot. Answer NO when the gripper is in delicate contact: grasping an object, or inserting/aligning it precisely. Also lean NO when the gripper is engaged on a handle or knob and pulling a hinged door or sliding drawer OPEN toward the robot — the arm is then moving under load along a fixed hinge/slide path, a constrained motion that should be protected. This applies to a single swinging door just as much as to a double door or drawer: read the direction from the gap, not from how many panels there are — if the gap behind the panel is WIDENING and the door edge or handle sweeps TOWARD the camera, it is opening under load, so NO; if the gap is NARROWING and the panel is moving AWAY from the robot, it is closing and unconstrained, so YES. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
