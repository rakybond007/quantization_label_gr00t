Compression is the default: answer YES whenever the arm is moving through free space — reaching, lifting after a grasp, transporting a grasped object across the frame, lowering it toward a wide/open landing area, releasing, and retracting. The long mid-trajectory travel between events is free motion even when the object is clearly heading toward a destination — compress it. Answer NO only at the two brief delicate-contact instants: the gripper closing around an object to grasp it, and settling an object into precise alignment or insertion with a specific target (fitting it to a referent, threading into a recess, exact placement relative to other objects). Do NOT extend that NO to the approach or the descent that precedes it — only the final alignment instant itself. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
