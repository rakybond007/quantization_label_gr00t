Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, broad sweeps, or pushing a surface or panel closed. Answer NO when the gripper is in delicate contact: grasping an object, inserting or aligning it precisely, OR when the gripper is hooked on a handle or knob and pulling an articulated part open along its constrained path — a hinged door swinging out toward the robot, or a drawer sliding open. Pushing something shut is free motion (YES); gripping and pulling it open is constrained contact (NO). Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
