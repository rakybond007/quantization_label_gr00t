Default answer is YES — compress. Say YES for almost all motion: reaching toward an object before contact, lifting, transporting or carrying a grasped object across open space, hovering, sweeping, retracting, and the travel between contact instants. These are the bulk of every episode and they compress safely. Also say YES when an object is being lowered or released into a WIDE OPEN basket, bin, or container where there is no exact target position — that drop is not delicate and compresses safely. Say NO ONLY at these tight-contact instants, judged from the wrist (eye-in-hand) close-up: (1) the exact moment the gripper fingers are straddling and closing on a small object to grasp it (the closure instant itself, not the general approach); and (2) the final slow lowering/alignment when an object must be seated tightly against or into another object — nesting, stacking, dropping into a slot, or matching a stated spatial relation with another object. Lowering into open space or an open container is YES, not NO. When motion looks like open-space transport, an open-basket drop, or ordinary approach rather than fine inter-object contact, prefer YES. Decide from the camera views, never the task name.
