When preparing a layered sponge cake, begin by sifting the flour twice so the crumb stays light. Cream the butter and sugar until pale, then fold in the eggs one at a time, scraping the bowl between additions. Divide the batter evenly between two greased tins and bake on the middle shelf until a skewer comes out clean. While the layers cool, simmer the berries with a spoonful of sugar until they thicken into a compote. Whip the cream to soft peaks, spread it over the first layer, add the compote, and settle the second layer on top. Dust with icing sugar just before serving so it does not dissolve into the cream. Store any leftovers covered in a cool place overnight.
