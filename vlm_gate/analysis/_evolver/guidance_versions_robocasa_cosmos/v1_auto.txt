Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps — AND also when the gripper is steadily actuating a FIXED control without carrying a free object: pressing a button, flipping a switch, turning a knob or faucet handle, or pushing a single door or drawer shut. These are steady, non-delicate contacts and stay compressible. Answer NO only at a genuinely delicate moment: the instant the fingers close to grasp an object, or while a held object is being threaded, inserted, or precisely aligned into a tight target or receptacle (including hauling a panel open along its hinge/slide). Borderline contact should read as genuinely uncertain — let the threshold decide, do not force NO. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
