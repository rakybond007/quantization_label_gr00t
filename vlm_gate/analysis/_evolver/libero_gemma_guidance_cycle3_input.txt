Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching toward an object, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps — and ALSO when lowering or releasing a grasped object into a wide, open, forgiving container such as a basket or bin, where the exact landing position does not matter. Answer NO only at moments of delicate contact or precise placement: closing the gripper to grasp an object, inserting or aligning it into a tight target, or setting it down onto a specific small spot or right next to a particular referent where its final position must be exact. Decide from the camera views, not the task name; use the wrist eye-in-hand close-up to spot the actual grasp-closure or fine-placement instant rather than general closeness.
