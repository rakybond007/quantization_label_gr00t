Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps. Also answer YES when lowering or releasing a grasped object into a WIDE open container or basket — a large drop zone where the object just has to land inside, with no precise alignment required. Answer NO only when the gripper is in delicate contact: closing around an object to grasp it, or inserting/aligning/placing it onto a specific spot or into a tight fit where exact position matters. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
