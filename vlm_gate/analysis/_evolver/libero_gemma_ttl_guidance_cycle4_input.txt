Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching toward a target, transporting a grasped object, retracting after release, empty-gripper travel between sub-steps of a longer task, or broad sweeps. In the wrist (eye-in-hand) view an object always looks close because the camera rides on the gripper; that closeness is NOT contact. Answer NO only at the delicate-contact INSTANT you can actually see: the fingers closing around an object in the grasp, or a held object being precisely inserted/aligned into its target. If you cannot see fingers closing or a precise insertion happening right now, treat it as free motion and answer YES. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
