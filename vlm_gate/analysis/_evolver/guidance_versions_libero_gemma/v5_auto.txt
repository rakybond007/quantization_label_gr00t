Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps — and also when lowering a grasped object into a wide open container, bowl, or basket where the exact landing spot does not matter. Answer NO when the gripper is in delicate contact: closing around an object to grasp it, inserting or threading it into a tight opening, OR making the final positional approach where a held object must be set down at a precise spot defined by other objects on the table — placing it directly next to, on top of, or precisely between nearby objects, the last centimeters where a small position error would miss the intended target. Decide from the camera views, not the task name: if the drop zone is a roomy container with slack, lean YES; if the object must be positioned exactly relative to other objects already on the table, lean NO. Treat borderline placements as genuinely uncertain and let the threshold decide.
