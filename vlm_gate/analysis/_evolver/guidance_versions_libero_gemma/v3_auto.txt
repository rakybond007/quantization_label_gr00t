Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching toward an object, transporting a grasped object, retracting, broad sweeps — or when releasing an object into a WIDE open container or basket where exact landing position does not matter. Answer NO only when the gripper is in delicate, position-critical contact: closing on an object to grasp it; inserting or aligning it precisely; or lowering and releasing a held object onto a SMALL specific target spot, or hard up against / right next to another object, where a few centimeters of placement error would miss the goal. In the close wrist view, when a held object is settling into its exact final resting place beside or on top of other objects and precise position matters, treat it as delicate (NO); when it is simply dropped into open space or a loose bin, keep it free (YES).
