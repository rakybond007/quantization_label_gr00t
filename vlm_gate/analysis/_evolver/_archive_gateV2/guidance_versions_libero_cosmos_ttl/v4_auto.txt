Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps. Releasing or lowering a grasped object into a WIDE-OPEN basket, bin, or container — one whose opening is clearly much larger than the object, so any drop inside lands successfully — is coarse placement, not precision: answer YES for it too. Answer NO only when the gripper is in delicate contact: closing to grasp an object, inserting/aligning it precisely, or setting it onto a specific small spot or nesting it against another object.
