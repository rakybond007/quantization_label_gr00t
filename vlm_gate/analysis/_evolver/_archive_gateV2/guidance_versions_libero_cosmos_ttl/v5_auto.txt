Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching toward an object, descending toward a grasp, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps. The reach-and-descent toward a grasp is still coarse free motion (YES); only the single instant when the fingers actually close on the object is delicate. Releasing or lowering a grasped object into a WIDE-OPEN basket, bin, or container — one whose opening is clearly much larger than the object, so any drop inside lands successfully — is coarse placement, not precision: answer YES for it too. Answer NO only when the gripper is in delicate contact: the fingers closing on an object to grasp it, inserting/aligning it precisely, or setting it onto a specific small spot or nesting it against another object.
