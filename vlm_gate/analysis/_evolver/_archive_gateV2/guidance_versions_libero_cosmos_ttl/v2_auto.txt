Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps — AND when it is lowering or releasing a carried object over a WIDE, forgiving landing zone (an open basket, bin, or clear stretch of counter) where the exact drop position does not matter. Answer NO only in the brief instants of delicate contact: the moment the fingers actually close around an object, or when placing/inserting/aligning it PRECISELY at a specific spot — into a slot or narrow target, or in a required position relative to another object. When in doubt during transport or an open, forgiving drop, prefer YES. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
