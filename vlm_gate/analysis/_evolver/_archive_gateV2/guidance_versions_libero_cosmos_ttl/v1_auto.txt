Compression is the default: answer YES whenever the arm is in open motion — reaching through free space, transporting a grasped object across the scene, retracting, or broad sweeps. Treat pure transit as a confident YES: if a grasped object is travelling with clear space around the fingertips and no target is yet close beneath them, that is free motion — this includes the early part of a descent toward a wide, open receptacle (a basket or open landing zone). Answer NO ONLY at the delicate instant itself: the frame where the gripper fingers actually close on an object, or where a fingertip is threading/aligning the object into a tight, narrow, or recessed fit. General closeness in the wrist view is not enough — protect the actual grasp-closure or fine-insertion moment, not the approach or the carry. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
