Default answer is YES — compress. Say YES for almost all motion: reaching toward an object before contact, lifting a grasped object, transporting or carrying it across open space, hovering, sweeping, and retracting. These are the bulk of every episode and compress safely. Note about the wrist (eye-in-hand) close-up: because that camera is mounted on the gripper, a grasped or nearby object ALWAYS looks large and close — this closeness is normal and is NOT by itself a reason to say NO. While an object is simply being held and carried in the gripper, answer YES. Say NO ONLY at two visually-confirmed contact instants, both judged from the wrist view: (1) the exact moment the gripper fingers are straddling and actively closing on an object to grasp it; and (2) the final slow lowering that seats an object into a TIGHT relationship with another object — nesting, stacking, dropping into a slot or opening, or aligning to a stated inter-object spatial relation. Everything else — all travel between those instants, and all mid-air carrying where the object is merely held in the gripper — is YES. When motion looks like transport or an object held in the gripper rather than fingers actually closing or an object being seated against another, prefer YES. Decide from the camera views, never the task name.
