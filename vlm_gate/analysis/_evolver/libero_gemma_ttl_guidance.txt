Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps. In particular, once a grasp is secured and the object is visibly lifted clear of the surface and traveling laterally — including a long relocation across the workspace between two placement spots in a multi-step task — that whole transit phase is YES; a held object hovering above the table in motion is free motion, not contact. Answer NO only in the brief instant of delicate contact: fingers closing to grasp, or the object being aligned/inserted precisely into its target. Decide from the camera views, not the task name.
