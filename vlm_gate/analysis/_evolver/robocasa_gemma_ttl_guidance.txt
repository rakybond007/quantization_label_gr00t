Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching toward an object before the gripper closes on it, transporting a grasped object toward a target, retracting after release, broad sweeps, or pushing a door or drawer closed. The grasp is only a brief window: answer NO just at the instant the gripper is closing on the object, or during a precise insertion/alignment — NOT during the open-air approach that precedes the grasp or the retract that follows the release, which are free-space YES. Also answer NO when the gripper is closed on a handle, knob, or drawer-pull and drawing a door or drawer OPEN, so the object moves with the gripper along a fixed hinge or slide arc — this constrained pull-open is not free space even though the arm appears to sweep. A steady in-place twist of a knob, faucet handle, or spout (rotation without translating the object) is free space (YES). Closing a single door or drawer with a push is still free space (YES). Decide from the camera views, not the task name; use the wrist view to pin the exact instant the gripper closes on the object or handle versus moving in open air — objects always look close in the wrist view, so closeness alone is not a precision cue.
