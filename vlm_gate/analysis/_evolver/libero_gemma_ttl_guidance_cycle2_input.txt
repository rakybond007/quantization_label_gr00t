Compression is the default: answer YES when the arm is moving through free space — reaching, transporting a grasped object, retracting, or broad sweeps. This also covers lowering and releasing a grasped object over a WIDE-OPEN basket, bin, or open surface, where it only has to drop inside a large target with no fine positioning — that carry-and-release is still free motion, YES. Answer NO only when the gripper is in delicate contact: closing the fingers around an object to grasp it, or inserting/aligning/setting an object down precisely against a specific spot, edge, or another object.
